Geopoly Game Rules

🌍 The 4 Continents & 40 Tiles

The board features four economic quadrants: Americas (Green), Africa (Orange), Europe (Blue), and Asia (Red). Property costs reflect real-world economic clout in descending or ascending tiers.

✈️ Continental Hub Airports

Each side boasts one international airport. Holding more airports doubles transit revenues when rivals land in your transit corridor ($25M → $50M → $100M → $200M).

🏛️ Consulates & Embassies

Once you own all countries in an economic pair or quadrant, develop Consulates (up to 2) and an Embassy to supercharge sovereign toll collection.

⚠️ Sanctions & Tariffs

Watch out for Import Tariffs ($200M) and Carbon Taxes ($100M). Landing on "Trade Sanction" sends you to diplomatic detention until you pay $50M or roll doubles.
