Geopoly Rules of Engagement

🏁 4 Corners & Special Zones:

  • WORLD BANK (Tile 0 - START): Pass or land to collect a $200M liquidity grant.
  • Diplomatic Detention (Tile 10): Trade Sanctions area with 4 exit options: $50M Sovereign Settlement, Roll Doubles (3 tries), UN Veto Card, or Bilateral Asylum ($30M to superpower).
  • Global Summit (Tile 20): Collects climate tax pool AND triggers an emergency UN General Assembly Resolution vote!
  • Diplomatic Crisis (Tile 30): Immediately sent to Trade Sanctions on Tile 10.

🌐 Continental Trade Blocs:

Unify an entire continent (all 6 countries) to unlock its Customs Union perk:

  • Americas (Pan-American Pact): Tariff Exemption & +20% base rent.
  • Africa (African Union): Consulates & Embassies cost 25% less.
  • Europe (European Union): Carbon Tax immunity & collect carbon levies.
  • Asia (BRICS+): India (#37) and USA/China (#39) receive +25% extra rent surcharge.

🏛️ IMF Bailouts & Austerity:

If cash dips negative, the IMF provides an emergency $300M credit line with a 50% garnishment on future rents. A second default under active loan results in total bankruptcy.

✈️ Continental Airports & Flight Network:

Owning airports scales toll revenue ($25M / $50M / $100M / $200M). Landing on any airport allows taking a charter flight for $50M to any other airport!
