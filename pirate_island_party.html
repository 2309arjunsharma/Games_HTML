Rules of the High Seas

1. Roll & Hop: Roll the 3D dice block to traverse the winding paths of Pirate Island.

2. Fork in the Road: Choose whether to brave the dangerous North Skull Rock shortcut or stroll along the safe South Sandy Cove.

3. Items & Satchel: Visit the Quartermaster's Shack to purchase Double Dice, Swap Hooks, and Plunder Gloves. Activate them before rolling!

4. Moving Treasure Star: Purchase a Power Star for 20 Doubloons. Once claimed, the chest relocates to another spot on the map!

5. Captain Blackbeard's Wrath: Landing on a Skull tile unleashes Doubloon Revolutions, position shuffles, or hefty coin penalties!

6. End-of-Round Mini-Games & Bonus Stars: Compete in 4 exciting mini-games after each round. At the grand finale, 3 hidden bonus stars are revealed to crown the Pirate King!
